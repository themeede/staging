Method

American closing prices were converted to implied probabilities and normalized so both sides sum to one. Accuracy d=5 scores favorites at 55%+ confidence; d=10 at 60%+. Log loss scores every forecast. ECE uses 20 fixed-width bins and 20 equal-observation/equal-liquidity-mass bins over favorite confidence.

Liquidity proxy = sqrt(valid quoted books) × numbered-PPV factor × main/co-main factor × title factor, normalized to mean one within year.
